Privacy Policy – Stock Pro 2.0

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Stock Pro 2.0 is an inventory and stock-management application provided by Oswal Classic. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Stock Pro 2.0 mobile application.

1. Information We Handle

The app uses account and user identifiers to authenticate users and provide inventory-management functionality. Depending on the account and features used, the service may also process business and inventory information such as barcode/article, shade, size, location, and quantity.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate users, maintain account access, synchronize inventory data, process stock-in and stock-out transactions, and provide the app's core functionality.

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

The app communicates with online backend services to authenticate users and synchronize inventory information. Data may be processed by backend and database service providers as necessary to provide the service.

4. Camera and Barcode Scanning

The app may request camera access for barcode scanning. Camera access is used for this feature and is not used for advertising or tracking.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information and do not use user identifiers for advertising or tracking purposes. Information may be processed by service providers operating the application's backend infrastructure as needed to provide and maintain the service.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the application. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Account and business information may be retained while an account is active or as needed to provide the service. For a deletion request, contact us at the email below. We may verify account ownership before processing the request.

8. Children's Privacy

Stock Pro 2.0 is a business inventory application and is not directed to children.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this location with a revised date.
